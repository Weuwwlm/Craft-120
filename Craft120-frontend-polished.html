Before you head our way

A few helpful details.

Quick answers to help you plan your visit.

Should I call ahead for a table?

Calling is the best way to ask about current table availability. You can also send a preferred date and time through the request form; the restaurant must confirm it before your reservation is final.

Is outdoor seating available?

The deck overlooking the Erie Canal is weather- and season-dependent. Please call ahead to check whether outdoor seating is available for your visit.

Where is the restaurant entrance?

Craft 120 is located behind Pontillo's Pizzeria at 120 E Union Street in Newark. Look for the Craft 120 restaurant entrance when you arrive.

Can I arrange a group gathering?

Yes, you can contact the team to discuss birthdays, family gatherings, business events and other celebrations. Send the event inquiry form or call (315) 573-7781 to talk through your plans.

Can I request food for pickup?

Use the menu to add dishes to a pickup request, then send it by text or email. The team will confirm availability, pricing and pickup timing. Your request is not an accepted order until Craft 120 replies.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Please call the restaurant to discuss ingredients, preparation and any dietary requirements before ordering. The team can confirm what is possible for the current menu.

Privacy & how requests work

This website does not submit reservation, event or pickup form details to a Craft 120 server. When you prepare a request, your device opens your email or messaging app with a draft; you choose whether to send it. The pickup list is saved locally in your browser so it can remain available if you return to this page. Do not enter sensitive personal information into request notes. Optional Google Analytics is disabled until a valid measurement ID is configured and you give consent. The restaurant should review this notice against its actual practices before publication.
